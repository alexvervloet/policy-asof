too, on purpose. A body is prose that never reaches the model; the clause text
does, so that is where the fence has to hold.

real effective date in its front matter, and a body that tries to talk its way
past both. Nothing in the body decides anything: the effective date comes from
the publisher's record, the fence markers are neutralised on ingest into the
prompt, and the marker token is per request so a document written today cannot
contain next week's.
