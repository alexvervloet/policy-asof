The stipend published in section 5.2 has been incorrect since the September 2025
review, which raised it to 55 EUR per month. The handbook was not updated at the
time. Payroll has been correct throughout; the published figure was not.

This is a correction to the record, not a change of policy. The entitlement from
1 September 2025 was always 55 EUR.
