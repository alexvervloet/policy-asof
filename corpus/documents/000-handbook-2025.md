sections:
  - section: "4.1"
    heading: Annual leave
    text: |
      Employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing
      monthly from the start date. Unused leave may be carried into the first
      quarter of the following year, after which it lapses. Leave is requested
      through the leave portal and approved by the employee's manager.
  - section: "4.2"
    heading: Parental leave
    text: |
      Employees are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the
      birth or adoption of a child. Leave may be taken in up to three separate
      blocks within the first 18 months. Pay during parental leave is 100% of
      base salary. Notice of at least 8 weeks is expected where practical.
  - section: "5.1"
    heading: Expense cap
    text: |
      Reimbursable expenses are capped at 75 EUR per day for meals and
      incidentals while travelling on company business. Receipts are required
      for any single item above 25 EUR. Expenses are submitted within 30 days
      of being incurred.
  - section: "5.2"
    heading: Remote work stipend
    text: |
      Employees working remotely for three or more days per week receive a
      stipend of 40 EUR per month towards home office costs. The stipend is
      paid with salary and is not conditional on receipts.
  - section: "6.1"
    heading: Commuting allowance
    text: |
      A temporary commuting allowance of 60 EUR per month is paid to employees
      travelling to the Antwerp office by public transport. This allowance is
      introduced for one year and expires on 31 December 2025.
    valid_to: 2026-01-01
  - section: "6.2"
    heading: Equipment allowance
    text: |
      Employees may claim up to 800 EUR every three years towards a desk,
      chair, monitor, or other home office equipment. Claims above 400 EUR
      require manager approval before purchase.

The 2025 edition of the employee handbook, in force from 1 January 2025.
